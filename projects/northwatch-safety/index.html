What We Do

Seven core areas built on real-world experience

Active Shooter Response Training

Customized, hands-on training that empowers your people to respond to an active threat with a specific plan and purpose.

Physical Security Consulting & Design

Expert evaluation and design of physical security measures for new construction, existing structures, and residential safe rooms.

Emergency Operations Plans

Comprehensive, site-specific emergency plans with clear protocols so your team knows exactly what to do when a critical incident occurs.

Security Vulnerability Assessments

A thorough evaluation of your facility's strengths and weaknesses, with actionable recommendations to close the gaps.

Tactical Response Training

Advanced tactical instruction for law enforcement, armed security, and church security teams operating in high-risk environments.

Medical Training

Life-saving medical response skills that prepare your team to treat critical injuries in the vital minutes before first responders arrive.

Keynote Speaking

Engaging, expert-led presentations on life safety and security preparedness for conferences, in-service days, and corporate events.
