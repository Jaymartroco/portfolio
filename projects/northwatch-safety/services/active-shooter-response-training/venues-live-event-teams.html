The Problem

Live Events Don’t Happen in Controlled Environments

Live event teams operate in constantly changing venues with different layouts, access points, crowd dynamics, and local resources. When a critical incident occurs, there is no time to learn a new building or improvise a response. Without training that prepares teams to think, communicate, and act under pressure, confusion spreads quickly and risk escalates.

Features & Benefits

What Training for Venues & Live Event Teams Includes

Venue-Agnostic Response Frameworks

Training emphasizes adaptable response principles that work across different venue types and layouts.

Tour Team–Focused Training

Instruction is tailored to the specific roles and needs of each tour, including tour managers, production leads, road crews, and support staff.

Crowd, Artist, & Crew Considerations

Programs address decision-making that considers artists, crews, vendors, and large crowds simultaneously.

Practical, Responsible Instruction

Training is delivered without fear-based messaging, ensuring teams leave confident and prepared.

Rapid Response Medical Training

Approximately 25% of our program focuses on life-saving medical response. Because first responders cannot render aid until the threat is neutralized, it's vital that your team is prepared to control bleeding and stabilize those who may be injured in those critical first minutes. This portion is specifically tailored for civilians and does not include graphic imagery.

Did you know? After-action reviews of large outdoor events consistently show that bystanders with basic bleeding-control training are often the first to give care, well before professional responders can reach the injured. A few hours of training can change outcomes.

What We Do

Active Shooter Response Training for Venues & Live Event Teams

Active shooter response training for venues and live event teams requires an approach that accounts for movement, crowds, and constantly changing environments. Northwatch Safety provides active shooter response training specifically designed for touring crews, production teams, and live event staff who operate across multiple venues and cities.

Rather than focusing on a single facility, training emphasizes adaptable decision-making, clear communication, and coordinated response that can be applied at amphitheaters, arenas, festivals, and temporary event sites. By grounding instruction in real-world incident analysis and operational experience, Northwatch Safety helps teams prepare to act decisively under pressure.

Live event organizations trust Northwatch Safety for training that is practical, responsibly delivered, and focused on protecting people without disrupting the experience audiences come to enjoy.

Do you offer online training?

No. Our hands-on, in-person approach is what makes our training so impactful. For clients who have completed our in-person training within the last two years, we offer an online refresher course to help reinforce their skills.

How do you price your training?

Our training is per-person on a sliding scale. Please contact us for details.

Is this training appropriate for all staff, including those uncomfortable with violence?

Yes. We have spent years learning our audience and have carefully tailored our programming with that in mind. While we cover the realities of active violence, we focus on practical strategies anyone can use to protect themselves and others. We do not traumatize participants, and there are no surprises.

What makes Northwatch Safety' training different from standard active shooter training programs?

Check out our What We Do page to learn how we developed the R.E.A.D.Y. curriculum and how we deliver our program with sensitivity and impact, setting us apart from standard active shooter training programs.
