The Problem

Schools Face Unique Responsibilities During Critical Incidents

Faculty and staff do not have the option of acting solely in self-preservation during an emergency. They are entrusted with the safety of students, often while navigating complex buildings, varied age groups, and rapidly changing situations. Generic response guidance fails to account for these realities, leaving educators underprepared for moments that demand clarity, coordination, and decisive action.

The Solution

Training Built for the Reality of School Environments

Northwatch Safety delivers active shooter response training that reflects how incidents actually unfold on school campuses. Our approach emphasizes decision-making, communication, and coordinated response, tailored to your facilities, student population, and staff roles. This is not a one-size-fits-all program. It is training designed to support educators so they can respond with confidence and care when it matters most.

Features & Benefits

What Training for Schools & Campuses Includes

Environment-Specific Guidance

Training addresses your specific concerns and operational realities, supported by an on-site walkthrough.

Staff-Focused Decision Frameworks

Faculty and staff learn how to assess situations, communicate clearly, and take appropriate action without relying on memorization or unrealistic scripts.

Prevention, Response, & Recovery

Programs address deterrence, immediate response, and post-incident considerations to support student safety and staff wellbeing.

Empowerment Without Fear

Instruction is delivered thoughtfully, emphasizing confidence and preparedness. Having trained educators in more than 320 schools nationwide, Northwatch Safety is trusted to support staff without causing distress. The focus is on practical readiness that empowers educators rather than overwhelms them.

Rapid Response Medical Training

Approximately 25% of our program focuses on life-saving medical response. Because first responders cannot render aid until the threat is neutralized, it's vital that staff be prepared to control bleeding and stabilize those who may be injured in those critical first minutes. This portion is specifically tailored for civilians and does not include graphic imagery.

What We Do

Active Shooter Response Training for Schools & Campuses

Active shooter response training for schools requires a fundamentally different approach than workplace or individual-focused programs. Faculty and staff are responsible not only for their own safety, but for the protection of students across classrooms, hallways, and campus spaces. Northwatch Safety provides active shooter response training designed to support educators in making clear, informed decisions during critical incidents.

Our training programs emphasize prevention, situational awareness, and coordinated response, tailored to each school’s facilities, student population, and operational structure. By focusing on decision-making rather than memorization, Northwatch Safety helps schools build preparedness that can be understood, practiced, and applied under stress.

Schools and campuses across the country trust Northwatch Safety for active shooter training that is realistic, responsibly delivered, and grounded in real-world experience rather than theoretical checklists.

Do you offer online training?

No. Our hands-on, in-person approach is what makes our training so impactful. For clients who have completed our in-person training within the last two years, we offer an online refresher course to help reinforce their skills.

How do you price your training?

Our training is per-person on a sliding scale. Please contact us for details.

Do you travel nationwide?

Yes!

What makes Northwatch Safety' training different from standard active shooter training programs?

Check out our What We Do page to learn how we developed the R.E.A.D.Y. curriculum and how we deliver our program with sensitivity and impact, setting us apart from standard active shooter training programs.

Is this training appropriate for all staff, including those uncomfortable with violence?

Yes. This training is designed with educators in mind and is delivered thoughtfully and responsibly. While the topic may be challenging or emotionally sensitive, the focus is on practical strategies and clear roles that help staff protect students and one another. The training avoids unnecessary graphic content, includes clear expectations, and is structured to support confidence and preparedness rather than fear.

I’m not sure if my organization needs training or another security solution. Can you help me decide?

Yes. We are more than happy to discuss your needs with no obligation and help you decide the next best step for your organization.

Why is training educators necessary?

You are a first responder. There is no waiting for the “good guys” to arrive. You have to act. Research shows that the first few minutes of an attack often determine the outcome, and by that time, professional responders may not yet be on the scene.  Our training equips educators with the skills and confidence to act decisively in critical moments, protecting themselves and students.

What ages is this appropriate for?

Our training is designed specifically for faculty and staff. We do not train students, because doing so creates security risks and safety concerns. We can provide guidance for specific groups or roles upon request.
