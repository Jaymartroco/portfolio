Our Courses

Tactical Emergency Casualty Care (TECC)

TECC focuses on and provides guidelines for managing trauma in the civilian tactical or hazardous environment. This two-day course takes an all-hazard approach to providing care outside the normal operating conditions of most EMS agencies, such as responding to a mass casualty or active shooter event.

Designed for anyone who may find themselves in a high-stress emergency situation, TECC is open to civilians, first responders, security personnel, and concerned community members alike. It is fully aligned with the recommendations of the Hartford Consensus (I, II, III, and IV), the C-TECC standards, and the principles of Tactical Combat Casualty Care.

Upon successful completion of TECC, students receive a 4-year certification from NAEMT as a TECC Provider.

CPR/AED Certification

The American Heart Association’s Heartsaver course equips participants to quickly recognize life-threatening emergencies, perform high-quality chest compressions, deliver effective ventilations, and use an AED.
‍
Upon successful completion of the course and skills assessment, you'll receive your CPR certification card via email, valid for two years and guaranteed to be accepted.

4-Hour Rapid Response Medical Training

This four-hour course provides hands-on medical training based on Tactical Emergency Casualty Care (TECC) principles in an accessible environment. Participants learn life-saving techniques for the two leading preventable causes of death in high-threat situations: massive hemorrhage and tension pneumothorax. Through classroom lesson and interactive skills practice, this course is suitable for civilians and professionals alike.

What We Do

Medical Training for Emergency Response & Trauma Care

Rapid response medical training plays a critical role in saving lives during emergencies. Northwatch Safety provides comprehensive medical training programs ranging from CPR/AED certification to Tactical Emergency Casualty Care (TECC). Our courses are designed to prepare individuals to respond effectively in high-stress situations where immediate medical intervention can make the difference between life and death.

By combining evidence-based instruction with hands-on practice, Northwatch Safety ensures participants gain the confidence and competence needed to deliver care under pressure. Training is tailored to the audience and environment, making it relevant for civilians, security teams, first responders, and organizations across industries.

Questions

Find answers about our rapid response medical training and what to expect.

Is prior medical experience required?

No.

Are the courses graphic or intense?

We offer TECC courses that include full, graphic scenario-based training, alongside a 4-hour Rapid Response Medical Training course. While the Rapid Response course may include limited graphic imagery, it does not involve the immersive, high-intensity scenarios used in TECC.

What skills will I learn?

In our TECC and 4-Hour Rapid Response Medical course, you’ll learn life-saving techniques for high-risk situations, including controlling massive hemorrhage and treating tension pneumothorax.

Which courses offer certifications?

Upon successful completion, our TECC and CPR courses offer certifications.
