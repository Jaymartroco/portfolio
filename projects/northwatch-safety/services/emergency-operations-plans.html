The Problem

Most Emergency Plans Live on Paper. Emergencies Don’t.

Many organizations have Emergency Operations Plans that are outdated, ineffective, or never fully implemented. When an incident occurs, teams are left searching for answers instead of executing a clear response. A plan that cannot be understood, practiced, or trusted becomes a liability when it matters most.

The Solution

Plans Built for Action, Not the Filing Cabinet

Northwatch Safety develops Emergency Operations Plans grounded in how emergencies actually unfold. Each plan is tailored to your environment, operations, and people, delivering clear guidance for response and recovery. The result is a plan your team understands, trusts, and can execute under pressure.

Features & Benefits

Core Components of a Northwatch Safety Emergency Operations Plan

Customized to Your Environment

Plans are built around your facilities, staffing structure, and operational realities.

All-Hazards Framework

Our all-hazards approach addresses a wide range of threats, including natural disasters, medical emergencies, security incidents, and active violence, tailored to your geographic location.

Practical & Actionable

Clear roles, responsibilities, and response actions are outlined so teams know exactly what to do when seconds matter—with optional training and resources to reinforce the plan.

NIMS Compliant

Our plans are written to be NIMS (National Incident Management System) compliant, which is the national standard across all emergency services, making for shared language and a seamless transition of authority when managing a crisis.

Not Sure Where to Start? We Can Help.

If your organization already has an Emergency Operations Plan, we can review and update it. If you don’t, we’ll help you build one from the ground up. Either way, we start by understanding your environment, your people, and your priorities.

What We Do

Emergency Operations Plans for Schools, Businesses, & Organizations

Emergency Operations Plans are critical tools for minimizing harm and restoring stability during unexpected events. Northwatch Safety develops comprehensive, customized EOPs that guide organizations through prevention, response, and recovery for a wide range of potential emergencies.

Our planning process goes beyond generic templates. We assess your facilities, operations, and risks to create a plan that is practical, clear, and usable under stress. Whether preparing for natural disasters, medical emergencies, security incidents, or active violence, our Emergency Operations Plans are designed to help teams act decisively and confidently when it matters most.
