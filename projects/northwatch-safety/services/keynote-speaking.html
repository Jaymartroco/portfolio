The Problem

Why This Topic Matters for Every Organization

Active violence response is not a niche concern. It is a leadership issue, a culture issue, and a continuity issue. Most organizations have plans on paper, but in high-stress moments, people default to confusion, hesitation, or assumptions that do not match reality.

A keynote can shift how an entire organization thinks - so readiness becomes shared, practical, and actionable. Brennan’s presentation includes insights that are directly applicable for:

‍

  • Business leaders focused on protecting people, reputation, and continuity
  • Church and community leaders responsible for welcoming environments without compromising safety
  • Human resources and operations teams tasked with managing risk, behavior, and crisis communication
  • Professional conferences and associations looking for relevant, engaging content that adds value for attendees
The Solution

A Keynote That Informs, Grounds, & Equips

Cole Brennan brings nearly two decades of law enforcement and real-world physical security experience into a presentation that is engaging, clear, and immediately useful. This is not a fear-based talk. It is a practical framework for how people and organizations can respond decisively during critical incidents, with insights that apply across industries and environments.

What We Do

Keynote Speaker on Active Shooter Response & Physical Security

Cole Brennan is a frequently booked keynote speaker on active shooter events, organizational response, and physical security. With nearly two decades of law enforcement experience and deep expertise in high-risk operations, Cole brings a calm, reality-based perspective to a topic that affects every organization.

These presentations are designed for leaders and teams who want more than awareness. Audiences leave with a clearer understanding of how critical incidents unfold, what readiness looks like in real life, and how to build a culture that supports decisive action under pressure.

Cole’s keynotes are a strong fit for professional conferences, associations, corporate leadership events, school safety audiences, healthcare systems, faith-based organizations, and community leaders seeking relevant, engaging content with practical takeaways.
