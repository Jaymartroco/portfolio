The Problem

Security Should be Part of the Plan, Not an Afterthought

Once construction is complete, security upgrades become costly, disruptive, and limited by existing infrastructure. Many buildings are designed for aesthetics or efficiency alone, unintentionally creating blind spots, access vulnerabilities, and response challenges. Addressing security during planning and construction eliminates these issues before they exist.

The Solution

Build Smarter, Safer, & Stronger From the Start

Northwatch Safety works alongside architects, builders, and developers to integrate physical security into new construction projects. By aligning design decisions with real-world security considerations, we help ensure buildings are intuitive to protect, easy to manage, and resilient to evolving risks from day one.

What We Do

Physical Security Consulting & Design for New Construction Projects

Physical security consulting for new construction allows organizations and homeowners to address risk before it becomes embedded in a building’s design. Northwatch Safety provides security consulting and design services for residential, commercial, and mixed-use construction projects, helping integrate protection into architecture, infrastructure, and site planning.

By evaluating access points, circulation paths, visibility, and technology integration during the planning phase, Northwatch Safety helps eliminate vulnerabilities that are costly or impossible to correct later. This proactive approach supports occupant safety, operational efficiency, and long-term resilience.

Builders, developers, and property owners trust Northwatch Safety for security consulting that is practical, collaborative, and grounded in real-world experience rather than reactive solutions.

How do your backgrounds inform your approach?

Cole’s two decades in law enforcement and high-risk operations, plus our hundreds of building assessments each year, give us insight into which security measures succeed, fail, or are difficult to integrate later. This experience ensures recommendations are realistic, effective, and long-lasting.

Do you recommend specific products or vendors?

We provide independent, vendor-neutral guidance. Our focus is on how to design and implement effective security, not on selling equipment or systems.

What is the process like?

Typical engagements include an initial consultation, a thorough site assessment, and a set of prioritized recommendations. We can also provide guidance for implementation and future reviews to adapt as needs evolve.

How do we get started?

You can request a consultation here. We’ll begin by discussing your goals, reviewing your project, and determining the best approach for your home or development.

We already have a developer or builder. Can you work with them?

Absolutely. We regularly collaborate with developers, builders, architects, and designers to integrate security seamlessly into the design and construction process. Our goal is to complement their work, providing guidance that enhances safety without disrupting the project.
