Gear Made for
Solar Glass.

Window-cleaning gear is not built for panels. Stiff brushes scratch, and unfiltered water leaves spots. We only use equipment designed for solar glass.

What's on the Truck

Robotic Cleaning

For big roofs and shade canopies, our cleaning robot covers hundreds of panels an hour with the same soft, even pressure, which keeps large jobs quick and consistent.

Purified Water

Valley tap water is loaded with minerals that leave white spots on glass. Our onboard filters strip those out, so panels dry clear without wiping or soap.

Soft Brushes & Safe Poles

Panels stay energized while we work, so our water-fed poles are lightweight and non-conductive. On the end are water-powered rotary brushes with bristles soft enough for anti-reflective coatings.

Real
Payback Math.

Shiny panels are nice, but the point is getting your power back. Here is what the research says about dust and output.

01

The Cost of Dust

The Research

A UC San Diego team measured a 7.4% average loss in output across 186 sites during a 145-day dry spell. Desert sites with heavy dust often lose more.

Put Another Way

One dusty summer can cost more output than fifteen years of normal panel aging.

The Region

National soiling maps from NREL show the desert Southwest among the hardest-hit regions in the country, thanks to fine airborne dust and long stretches without rain.
