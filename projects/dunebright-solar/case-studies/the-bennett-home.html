The Problem

Summer storms had cut power to the Bennett home several times. With a family member who depends on medical equipment, every outage was stressful.

They wanted dependable backup without a noisy, fuel-hungry generator.

The Plan

The essential circuits were mapped first, then the battery and panels were sized to carry them for several days.

  • 13 kW of panels on storm-rated racking
  • 30 kWh of home battery storage
  • A backup panel for the circuits that matter most
  • Automatic switchover the moment the grid drops

The Outcome

When an August storm knocked out the neighborhood, the house switched to battery on its own and ran for three days until the grid came back.
