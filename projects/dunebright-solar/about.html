Where It Started

Marcus Hale spent twelve years on utility-scale solar sites across the Southwest, first on install crews and later running them. He learned how panels are built, how they fail, and how to keep a crew safe on a live electrical site in 115-degree heat.

From Job Site to Back Office

He then moved into facilities management, overseeing safety and maintenance programs for property portfolios across the Valley and finishing an MBA in operations management at night.

What That Means for You

Too many cleaning outfits show up with a garden hose and no paperwork. Marcus started Dunebright to give homeowners and property managers a better option: trained crews, proper insurance, and a written report after every visit, whether the job is twelve panels or twelve thousand.

How We Work:
Safe Crews, Straight Answers,
Careful Work.

Good service starts with a team that likes where it works. We pay fairly, train constantly and keep the chain of command short, so the people on your roof care about doing the job right.

Three Things We Never Skip
01

Safety on Every Roof

Every visit starts with a site walk and a short safety briefing. Crews check tie-off points, roof condition and live wiring before anyone climbs, and they wear the right gear every time. It protects our people, your property and you.

02

Crews Who Work Together

Nobody on a Dunebright crew is too senior to coil a hose. Every technician owns the result, speaks up when something looks wrong, and treats a small home roof with the same care as a parking-garage canopy.

03

Training That Never Stops

New hires shadow senior techs for weeks before they lead a job, and the whole team trains every quarter on equipment, electrical safety and lift operation. You get people who understand how a PV system works, not just how to rinse it.

Coverage Built for
Commercial Sites

Property managers and general contractors can request our certificates before the first visit. Our coverage includes:

  • State Licensed & Registered
  • General Liability Insurance
  • Umbrella Liability Coverage
  • Commercial Vehicle Insurance
  • Workers' Compensation for Every Employee

OSHA 30 Trained Crews

Every crew lead holds OSHA 30 training in fall protection, hazard recognition and work around energized equipment, and every technician completes OSHA 10 before their first job.

ISCA Certified Technicians

Our technicians are trained through the International Solar Cleaning Academy on panel handling, approved cleaning methods and roof safety, so the way we clean lines up with what your manufacturer expects.

Aerial Lift Qualified Operators

Our field team is certified on scissor and boom lifts, which lets us reach tall carports and hard-to-access arrays safely instead of improvising with ladders.
