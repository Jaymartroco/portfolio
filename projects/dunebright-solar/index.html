Questions,
Answered

How often should panels be cleaned?

It depends on what is around your home, how steep your roof is and how often storms roll through. For most homes in the Valley we suggest two to three cleanings a year (more if you are near open desert, a busy road or a spot pigeons love).

Will your water leave spots?

Only purified, deionized water. We filter out the minerals that make Phoenix tap water so hard, so the glass dries clear with no white spots and no need to wipe.

Will your brushes scratch the glass?

No. Our soft rotary brushes and our cleaning robot are built specifically for solar glass. The bristles are softer than a car-wash brush and are always used with plenty of water, so dirt lifts off instead of grinding in.

Do you use soap?

Almost never. If something will not come off with water alone, we ask first and use a biodegradable soap made for solar glass.

Could cleaning affect my warranty?

Done the right way, cleaning helps protect it. We use the soft brushes, pure water and no-pressure methods that panel makers recommend, and we document every visit in case you ever need to file a claim.

If your panels have unusual requirements, send us the model and we will check the manual before we schedule.

Do I need to be home?

Not at all. We sort out gate codes and roof access ahead of time, then text you a report with before-and-after photos when we are done.

Won't the monsoon rain clean them?

A big storm moves some dust around, but it usually leaves a muddy film along the bottom edge. Light showers can also leave streaks and spots once the water evaporates. A proper cleaning takes all of it off.

What if a storm hits right after you clean?

If a storm leaves your panels visibly dirty within 30 days, we come back and clean them again for free.
