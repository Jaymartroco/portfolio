Our History

Origins

Veylor was founded in 2007 by a small group of infrastructure investors who believed essential mid-sized businesses were being overlooked. Since then we have built one of the more experienced independent teams in the sector.

Over the past decade our focus has sharpened around a handful of sectors where we know the assets, the operators and the risks. That focus shapes every investment we make today.
