About us

Making space transparent

Apsis exists to make any image in the Solar System available on demand. We are building the intelligence layer that makes more space activity possible.

Our core beliefs

Space should be transparent

You can't understand space if you can't see what's happening. Better information gives everyone a clearer picture of what is happening in orbit.

Space should be easy

Space shouldn't require weeks of expert analysis to answer a simple question. We use software, AI, and imagery to make those answers faster and easier to reach.

Space should be sustainable

We can't just lift infinite amounts of spacecraft into orbit. We need to do more with what's already out there.

Dr. Mara Ellison
Co-Founder & CEO
