Appointment & Salon Policies

Effective date: January 6, 2026

Appointment Booking 

A card on file is required to reserve all appointments. You won’t be charged until your visit.‍

Pricing Notice

Prices are listed as starting points and can vary by stylist level, hair length and density. If you’d like a firmer number before you commit, just ask — your stylist will walk you through a breakdown during your consultation and work with your budget.

Our front desk team is also happy to give you a more detailed estimate over the phone.

Cancellations & No Shows

At Larkspur, we value your time and ours. Please give us at least 24 hours’ notice if you need to cancel or reschedule. Cancellations inside 24 hours and missed appointments are charged 50% of the reserved service total. Thank you for understanding — it helps us take care of every guest.
