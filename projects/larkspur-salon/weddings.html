‍all things bridal

Contact Us
Contact Us
How far in advance should I book?

Reach out as soon as you have a date. Popular weekends, especially in spring and autumn, book up quickly, and enquiring early gives you the best chance of securing your preferred date and artists.

Do you require a deposit or contract?

Yes. To reserve your date we ask for a signed bridal agreement and a 35% retainer. Once both are received, your date and team are confirmed.

Do you travel for on-site services?

Absolutely — we’d love to join you wherever you’re celebrating. Travel within 25 miles of the salon is included. Beyond that, a travel fee of $1.25 per additional mile applies.

Should I book a trial?

We strongly recommend a trial. It’s the best way to collaborate with your artist, refine your hair and makeup and make sure every detail feels right. Many brides book their trial on the day of a shower, engagement shoot or rehearsal so they can enjoy the finished look.
