Good to Know

Before you
arrive

A few things to know before your visit, so your time in the chair feels easy from the moment you arrive.

01

Brows & Lash Services

Please arrive with a clean, makeup-free face. For lash services, remove contact lenses beforehand. Some services may not be recommended during pregnancy — ask us if you’re unsure.

02

Blowouts & Styling (No Wash)

Please arrive with clean, damp hair for no-wash blowouts and clean, dry hair for styling appointments.

03

Head Spa & Smoothing Services

So we can reserve the right amount of time, please call (615) 555-0148 to schedule your appointment.

04

Color, Highlight, & Select Blowout Services

Because every guest’s hair is different, pricing can vary with length, density and time needed.

05

Smoothing Treatment

A consultation is required before booking so we can make sure this service is right for your hair and goals. Please call (615) 555-0148 to schedule your consultation.
