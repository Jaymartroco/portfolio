Pixel Arcade | Downtown Street Team

CLIENT
Pixel Arcade
LOCATION
Denver, CO (Downtown Entertainment District)
YEAR
Q4 2025

Overview

Pixel Arcade partnered with NOCTA to test a high-energy street team in a busy downtown nightlife district, aiming to boost awareness and sign-ups for an in-venue retro gaming tournament. NOCTA staffed and managed a branded street team to reach visitors directly in one of the city’s most competitive entertainment strips.

‍

The Challenge

  • Standing out in a crowded, noisy nightlife environment
  • Building awareness for a tournament tied to an in-venue experience
  • Grabbing attention fast and explaining the value in seconds

The Approach

  • Deployed a two-person NOCTA street team trained in quick, guerrilla-style engagement
  • Ran activations along the district during peak evening foot traffic
  • Used a fast, interactive mini-game to stop passersby and start a conversation
  • Handed out printed tournament cards and discounted play passes

‍

The Outcome

Across two activations, NOCTA created more than 500 face-to-face conversations and consistently held attention in a highly competitive setting. Same-night sign-ups were modest, but the test confirmed strong interest from visitors and gave Pixel Arcade clear direction on messaging, timing and formats for future tournaments.
