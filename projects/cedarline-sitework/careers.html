Join the Cedarline Crew

At Cedarline Sitework Group, the work is about more than punching a clock. Every pad we grade and every line we lay helps our communities grow responsibly. Each team member owns their part of the job, whether they're in the office, in the shop or in the cab.

As a family-owned company, we know our success depends on the people who show up every morning. We invest in training, promote from within and work hard to keep a close, supportive crew culture. If that sounds like the place for you, we'd love to hear from you.

Competitive Employee Benefits

We offer competitive pay, health coverage and a retirement plan with a company match, along with steady year-round work, well-maintained equipment and a schedule that respects your time at home.

When you join us, you're joining a crew that's known in the community for doing the job right.
