Who We Are

Cedarline Sitework Group is a locally owned site prep, utilities and earthwork contractor serving commercial, municipal and development clients across Central Georgia. Builders keep calling us back because we show up ready, work clean and leave behind sitework that holds up for decades.

Our Mission

Prepare, grade and service every site with care, precision and accountability, so the people building on it can start strong.

Our Vision

To be the sitework partner Central Georgia builders trust first, known for steady crews, honest schedules and
relationships that outlast any single project.

Why We Started Cedarline

Cedarline Sitework Group was founded in 2018 by people who had spent years on the other side of the fence, waiting on subcontractors who missed dates and stopped returning calls. We set out to be the crew we always wished we could hire: clear scopes, fair pricing and work that passes inspection the first time. Those same principles still shape every bid we send.

A Family-Owned Company

We're a family-owned company rooted in Harlow Springs, so our name is on every pad we grade and every pipe we lay. That accountability is why a lean, experienced team can take on complex, multi-phase work and still finish on time and on budget. Partner with us and you get a contractor your neighbors already know for doing it right.

The People Behind It

Darius and Renee Holloway
Founders

Darius and Renee Holloway grew up a few miles apart in rural south Georgia, where weekends meant fixing fences, hauling hay and finishing what you started. They have been married for more than twenty years and raised three kids who all spent summers riding along to job sites.

Darius started running equipment straight out of high school and spent fifteen years with regional civil contractors, working his way from laborer to operator to site superintendent on highway widening, subdivision and utility projects.

In 2012 he bought his first dump truck and started Red Clay Hauling, growing it into a dependable fleet that general contractors across Central Georgia relied on for fill, stone and haul-off.

As customers kept asking for more than hauling, Darius and Renee launched Cedarline Sitework Group in 2018. Renee leads finance and administration while Darius runs estimating and field operations, and together they still visit every active site each week.

Caleb Turner
Operations Supervisor

Caleb grew up on his family's cattle farm outside Harlow Springs and learned early how much good drainage and a well-built road matter. He earned a degree in construction management and spent several years as a field engineer before joining Cedarline.

As Operations Supervisor, Caleb oversees projects from pre-construction meetings through final grade. He schedules crews and equipment, coordinates with engineers, inspectors and general contractors, and keeps a close eye on safety and quality on every site.

Caleb's calm, hands-on style keeps crews focused and clients informed. His attention to detail is a big reason Cedarline consistently delivers predictable results, from the first stake to the final grade.

Jada Holloway
People & Communications Director

Jada is the eldest daughter of founders Darius and Renee Holloway and grew up hearing the company's values around the dinner table: show up, tell the truth and take care of people. Today she leads hiring, onboarding and client communications at Cedarline Sitework Group.

Jada builds the kind of workplace where crew members feel supported and clients always know what's happening on their project. Her work keeps Cedarline's relationships as solid as the ground we prepare.
