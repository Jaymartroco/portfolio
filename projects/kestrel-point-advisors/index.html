Common Questions

Whom do we serve?

We typically work with privately held companies between 10–1,000 employees. If you're not sure whether you're a fit, just reach out.

Are you a fiduciary?

Yes. Kestrel Point is a registered investment adviser and acts in a fiduciary capacity for all of its clients.

How do you work with employees directly?

We provide education, enrollment and rollover support for all employees. For those employees approaching retirement we have a specific and ongoing service offering to help with that transition.

What does it cost?

Our fees are based on plan size and complexity, typically charged as a percentage of plan assets. We're transparent about what you're paying and what you're getting.
