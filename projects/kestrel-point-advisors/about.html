The Story

How We Got Here

Owen and Daniel each spent close to two decades in financial services. Owen came from institutional portfolio management, while Daniel built his career consulting with plan sponsors for a national recordkeeper. They reached the same conclusion: company retirement plans are too often treated as an afterthought.

Most advisory firms are built around individual wealth management. If they oversee a 401(k) at all, it's often as a favor to a client. The plan is set up once and left on autopilot. Nobody is benchmarking the fees, coordinating the vendors or checking whether the lineup still fits the workforce.

Kestrel Point was founded to change that. We give retirement plans the structure and attention they deserve but rarely receive. We also manage personal wealth for business owners, their families and plan participants, but the retirement plan is where every relationship begins.
